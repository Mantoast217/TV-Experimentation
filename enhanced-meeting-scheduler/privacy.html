Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Meeting Scheduler is a browser-only tool. There is no account, no login, and no server that stores your data — everything you do on the page (adding time zones, picking a date and time) happens locally in your browser and is gone when you close or reload the tab.

What we collect

The scheduling tool itself collects nothing — no accounts, no cookies, and no tracking scripts run while you're adding time zones, picking a date, or finding a time. Two things outside the tool itself can involve data, described below: the feedback form, and this site's hosting-level web analytics.

What leaves your browser

  • If you choose Outlook, Microsoft Teams, or Google Meet from the "Set up invite" menu, this app opens that provider's own compose page in a new tab, with the meeting title, the generated time summary (the city names and local times you see on screen), and the start/end time included in that page's URL. From that point on, Microsoft's or Google's own privacy policy applies to what they do with it — Microsoft's privacy statement and Google's privacy policy.
  • Download .ics does not send anything anywhere — it generates a calendar file on your own device.
  • The Feedback link in the footer opens a separate Google Form in a new tab. Using it is entirely optional. Anything you submit there — your responses, and any technical details Google Forms collects automatically when you fill in a form — is stored by Google Forms and may be reviewed by us for user research and to guide design improvements to this tool. Google's own privacy policy applies to the form itself — Google's privacy policy.

Hosting and web analytics

This site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Like effectively any web host, Cloudflare's infrastructure processes basic request-level information (e.g. IP address, browser type, requested page) to serve the page and to protect it from abuse — this is standard for any website and isn't specific to this tool.

If Cloudflare Web Analytics is enabled for this site, it's the cookie-less, no-persistent-identifier variant Cloudflare offers: it reports aggregate, anonymized traffic metrics — page views, approximate (country-level) location, referrer, and browser/device type — and doesn't build a profile of you or track you across other websites. See Cloudflare's Web Analytics page for how it works.

Data retention

The scheduling tool itself retains nothing — not in a database, not in cookies, not in your browser's local storage. Refreshing the page resets everything you entered. Feedback form responses (see above) are retained separately, in Google Forms, for as long as needed for the research and design purposes described above.

Changes to this policy

If this ever changes (for example, if we add accounts in the future), this page will be updated first.
