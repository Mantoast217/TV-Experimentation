Accessibility statement

Last updated: October 2026

This statement applies to Meeting Scheduler, including its Time zone finder page. We want as many people as possible to be able to use it. For example, you should be able to:

  • use every part of it with only a keyboard
  • zoom in up to 400% without text spilling off the screen or needing to scroll sideways
  • switch between light and dark mode
  • read all text with enhanced colour contrast (WCAG level AAA)

How accessible this site is

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. We're not aware of any parts of the site that fail to meet it.

We have not yet tested the site with people who use screen readers or other assistive technology. Until we do, there may be problems we haven't found.

Reporting problems

If you find a problem or need information in a different format, tell us using the feedback form (opens in new tab).

How we tested this site

The site was last tested on 8 October 2026. We tested every page, in both light and dark mode, using:

  • automated checks with axe-core 4.14 against WCAG 2.2 level A, AA and AAA rules
  • keyboard-only navigation, checking that every control can be reached and shows a visible focus indicator
  • a 320 pixel wide screen, which is equivalent to 400% zoom
